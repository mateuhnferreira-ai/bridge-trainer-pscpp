⚓ Core Berthing Vocabulary

Berth — atracar / berço.

Alongside — atracado ao cais.

Head line — lançante de proa.

Stern line — lançante de popa.

Breast line — espringue transversal / cabo transversal.

Spring — espringue.

Heaving line — retinida.

Heave on — colher / virar o cabo.

Slack away — dar brando.

Hold on — manter / segurar.

Make fast — fazer firme.

📝 Show Complete Transcript

Have the heaving lines ready forward and aft.

Send the head line ashore.

Send the stern line ashore.

Send out the aft spring.

We have to move five metres astern.

We are in position.

Make fast fore and aft.

Heave on the forward spring.

Pick up the slack on the stern line.

Slack away the head line.

Hold on the aft spring.

Keep the breast lines tight.

We are in position.

Make fast fore and aft.
