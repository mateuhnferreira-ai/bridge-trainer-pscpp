Ship controllability includes several different qualities.

Coursekeeping depends on dynamic stability and rudder effectiveness.

Turning ability can be assessed using advance, transfer and tactical diameter.

Yaw-checking ability is important because the shiphandler must be able to control an established turn.

📘 Technical Paragraph 2 — Shallow Water

What is the central idea?





📝 Show Transcript

As a ship enters shallow water, hydrodynamic conditions change.

Resistance can increase, sinkage and trim may change, and manoeuvring characteristics can deteriorate.

If the waterway is also restricted laterally, these effects may become more pronounced.

🏆 Final Technical Listening Challenge

Selecione Technical.

Ouça o texto inteiro uma única vez.

Select the complete technical interpretation.





📝 Show Complete Transcript

A large ship entering a shallow restricted channel may experience important changes in manoeuvring behaviour.

Resistance can increase and the vessel may lose speed for the same available power.

Squat can reduce under-keel clearance, particularly as speed increases.

Nearby banks can create asymmetric hydrodynamic forces that affect steering.

At low speed, rudder effectiveness may also depend strongly on propeller inflow.

For the shiphandler, careful speed control and continuous monitoring of the vessel's response are therefore essential.
